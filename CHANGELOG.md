# Changelog

What the "What's new" window shows. Each entry is one line starting with "- ": plain text, no "|", no braces, no links.
Every entry needs a translation in all 16 languages (Clink/Locales/<language>/changelog.json, key "whatsnew|" + the entry text).
The newest section comes first: "## Unreleased" (dev builds only), then "## X.Y.Z" or "## X.Y.Z - YYYY-MM-DD".

## Unreleased
- Record from a capture card: choose it under Source on the Capture tab. Its HDMI audio goes on the Game track, and Clink falls back to the screen when the card is missing.
- New option to follow the Windows main display for the captured screen.
- Trim: Save & share now opens the Share window.
- Library: Share is a split button. Drag and drop is the default; the other options are in its menu.
- Recording is smoother and more reliable. Updates and settings import are hardened.
- A What's new window shows what changed after an update. Open it again from the version menu.

## 0.4.8
- Clink releases are now code-signed.
